### 5. Silver Bow County, MT-CD01 (Zip: 59701)

**Complexity Rank: 89th Percentile**

While not as lengthy as some others, this Montana ballot's complexity comes from the unique and significant nature of its local races, which ask voters to make foundational decisions about their own government. The ballot features 27 questions, with 24 distinct races.

The most interesting and unusual item is the election for the **"Anaconda-Deer Lodge County Local Government Study Commission."** This is not a typical race for an operational office; instead, voters are asked to select five representatives from a field of thirteen candidates. This commission is tasked with studying the very structure of the local government and potentially recommending changes to its charter. This requires a high level of voter engagement, as the choice of commissioners could have long-term consequences for how the county is run.

Furthermore, the ballot includes three statewide initiatives (CI-126, CI-127, and CI-128) on fundamentally important topics: implementing a top-four primary system, requiring a majority vote to win elections, and establishing a right to abortion. These are not minor policy tweaks but significant changes to the state's political and legal landscape. The combination of these high-stakes initiatives with the unique, governance-focused local commission race makes this a particularly consequential and complex ballot for voters to prepare for.
