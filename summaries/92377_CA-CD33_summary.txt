### 3. San Bernardino County, CA-CD33 (Zip: 92377)

**Complexity Rank: 99th Percentile**

This ballot is a dual challenge, combining high-stakes, statewide policy decisions with a dizzying array of hyper-local, non-partisan races. Its top-tier complexity comes from forcing the voter to switch between broad, ideological questions and granular, community-level decisions across **49 different questions**.

The most significant feature is the slate of **13 ballot measures**. Ten of these are statewide propositions with profound consequences, covering contentious issues like the right to abortion (Prop 3), the minimum wage (Prop 32), local rent control (Prop 33), and criminal justice reform (Prop 36). These require voters to read, understand, and take a position on complex and often emotionally charged topics.

At the same time, the ballot drills down to an intensely local level. A voter must make decisions on 36 different races, many of which are for obscure but important non-partisan boards. These include the "Arrowbear Park County Water District Board," the "Daggett Community Services District Board," and the "Mojave Water Agency Board." These races often feature multiple candidates and receive little media coverage, placing the onus of research squarely on the voter. The ballot for the "East Valley Water District," for example, asks voters to choose two directors from a field of seven candidates. This blend of major statewide initiatives and a high volume of low-information local races makes this an exceptionally demanding ballot.
