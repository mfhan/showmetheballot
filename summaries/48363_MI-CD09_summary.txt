### 2. Oakland County, MI-CD09 (Zip: 48363)

**Complexity Rank: 99th Percentile**

The ballot in Oakland County is a prime example of complexity driven almost entirely by an exhaustive number of elected positions, featuring **48 distinct races** and no ballot measures. While the lack of measures might seem like a reprieve, the cognitive load is transferred to the immense task of researching candidates for a wide array of state and local offices.

The most striking feature is the number of non-partisan races with a high volume of candidates, which demands significant voter preparation. For instance, voters must select one judge for the Michigan Supreme Court from a field of four, and another for the 6th Circuit Court from a field of **eight candidates**. These non-partisan judicial races require voters to look past party affiliation and evaluate candidates on their individual merits, a time-consuming endeavor.

This pattern continues with education-related boards. The ballot includes races for the Michigan State Board of Education (8 candidates for 2 seats), the University of Michigan Board of Regents (7 candidates for 2 seats), the Michigan State University Board of Trustees (8 candidates for 2 seats), and the local Rochester Board of Education (10 candidates for 3 seats). Preparing for this ballot requires not just an understanding of top-ticket races, but a deep dive into the qualifications of dozens of individuals running for positions that have a major impact on state and local governance, particularly in education.
